ltr-forage · Chinese lotus (Nelumbo nucifera, GCF_000365185.1)

Lotus novel LTR candidates

These are single-element families from the lotus recipe library that pass every metric and match no LTR in the published lotus library (Jiang lotus 3.3, from the LTR_retriever paper). For each one, the page shows the structural evidence ltr-forage found and how close the nearest published family is.

Most "novel" lotus singletons were assembly-gap artifacts. 1,060 of the 1,191 candidates had LTRs that were mostly N: ltr-forage matched two assembly gaps to each other as if they were identical LTRs. This bug is now fixed (N never matches anything, as in GenomeTools), and the lotus recipe is being rerun. The 130 elements with N-free LTRs, including the eight below, are not affected. Four of the eight still have gaps inside their internal region, shown as hatched blocks.

The genome's own tRNAs

How these were measured

Structure (TSD, end motif, PBS, PPT, LTR identity) comes from ltr-forage's feature table. The TSD is the duplicated sequence just outside both LTRs: an exact repeat, or failing that a 5–6 bp repeat with one mismatch directly against both LTR ends. In lotus a one-mismatch repeat sits at the ends of 20% of TG…CA elements that lack an exact TSD, against 1% in flanks 37 bp further out. The PPT is computed on the + strand. The PPT is the most purine-rich 15 bp window in the 30 bp before the 3′ LTR; 11 or more purines is suggestive.

Primer binding site. tRNA genes were called in the lotus genome with ARAGORN (now ported into ltr-forage). Each tRNA's 3′ end, its last 15 nt plus the CCA added after transcription, gives a probe: the reverse complement of its first 12 nt, which starts with TGG. A PBS is a probe match, with TGG exact and at most one other mismatch, starting from 5 bp inside to 15 bp past the inner edge of the 5′ LTR. Both strands are searched, so an element on the − strand has its PBS inside the 3′ LTR as drawn. "Paired" counts complementary bases over 18 nt. In random lotus windows of the same size an exact 12-nt match occurs 0.2% of the time and a match with up to one mismatch 3.6% of the time, so a hit is strong evidence.

Domain positions come from hmmscan with LTR_retriever's TEfam.hmm (Pfam retrotransposon models) on six-frame ORFs of at least 60 aa. Overlapping weaker hits were dropped.

Nearest published family: the 5′ LTR and the internal region were each searched with blastn (word size 11, E ≤ 1e-5) against the Jiang lotus 3.3 library, and the internal region with tblastx against its internal sequences. Identity is weighted over all alignments to that family. Coverage is the fraction of the query those alignments cover. The same LTR search was run against the rest of our own lotus library.

Genome copies are RepeatMasker hits of the LTR record covering at least half its length, from the whole-genome mask with the recipe library. The higher of the two LTRs is shown, and the count includes the element itself.
